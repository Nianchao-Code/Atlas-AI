# North America office appendix

Seattle office is near 500 Mercer St (ask IT for the floor; do not put it in external material). Timezone: Pacific Time.

## Overtime and leave

Washington overtime rules differ from China. North America full-time staff:

- Annual leave starts at **15 days**, matching China year one so the two HQs stay aligned
- Office closed Thanksgiving Thursday and Friday
- Sick leave accrues under Washington Paid Sick Leave. **Do not** apply the China 10-day cap

## Cross-timezone on-call

For K-Fleet North America customers, the Seattle duty manager is **incident commander** on SEV-1. Shenzhen provides robot firmware support. Handoff window is **16:00–18:00 PT** daily.
