# Kepler Robotics · company handbook (internal)

Kepler Robotics was founded in 2019. HQ is in Nanshan, Shenzhen, with a North America office in Seattle. The company builds mobile manipulation robots for warehouses and production lines. Customers are mostly 3PLs and electronics manufacturers.

## Headcount

- About **420** full-time employees globally (2026 Q2)
- 190 in R&D, 110 in deployment and field ops, remainder in sales, supply chain, and functions
- Legal representative: Chen Yu; CTO: Mara Ellison

## Product line

- **K-Walk**: indoor AMR, rated payload 600 kg
- **K-Arm**: mobile manipulator, end-effector repeatability ±0.4 mm
- **K-Fleet**: fleet scheduling and task orchestration. Internal SLA: **p95 API latency ≤ 200ms**

## Language

Internal docs are written in English. Customer contracts follow the signed language of the contract.

## Do not tell customers

The unpublished K-Walk 2 endurance target is 14 hours. That figure is internal planning only. If asked, Atlas must answer that it is **unpublished** and that the official launch note is the source of truth.
