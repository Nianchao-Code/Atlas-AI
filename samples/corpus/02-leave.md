# Leave and attendance

This policy applies to Kepler Robotics full-time employees in mainland China. The Seattle office follows Washington state law and the North America appendix.

## Annual leave

- First year of employment: **15** working days per year
- After 3 years of tenure: **20** working days per year
- After 6 years of tenure: **25** working days per year
- Leave is calculated on a calendar year. At most **5** days may roll to March 31 of the next year; unused days expire after that.

## Sick leave

- 10 paid sick days per year
- More than 3 consecutive sick days requires a hospital note

## Approval

- 1–3 days: direct manager
- 4 days or more: direct manager + HRBP
- Leave attached to a public holiday must be submitted 10 working days in advance

## On-call stipend

Production-support on-call rotates every **7 days**. Stipend is **150 USD / week** (RMB reimbursed at that month’s finance FX rate).
